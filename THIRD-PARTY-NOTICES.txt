v-pad helper — third-party notices

this program ships as a single self-contained file. the assemblies
listed below are redistributed inside it, each under its own licence.
the licence texts are reproduced in full because that is what they ask
for; everything else here is written by us.

── the gamepad driver ──────────────────────────────────────────────

this executable does not contain a driver, and neither does its
installer. the virtual gamepad is created by vigembus, a kernel driver
by nefarius software solutions e.u., which you install yourself from
the vendor's own release — the tray menu links straight to it.

we deliberately do not carry, repackage, or re-sign that installer.
it reaches you exactly as its authors published it, with its
original signature intact, which is the only way a kernel driver
can be trusted to load.

clause 2 of the bsd 3-clause licence binds anyone redistributing in
binary form. we no longer redistribute vigembus, so the text below is
reproduced as a credit rather than as a condition — the driver's own
installer carries its licence to you.

this program is not endorsed by or affiliated with nefarius software
solutions e.u., and no trademark of theirs is claimed here.
